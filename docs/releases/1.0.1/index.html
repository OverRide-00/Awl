Patch update · 1.0.1

Your wallpaper,
your colors.

Awl 1.0.1 fixes a template color lock: after you select a different Wallpaper Engine wallpaper and reload, Awl now draws its colors from that wallpaper instead of the previous template.

What changed

Choosing a custom wallpaper in Awl also deactivates the active template. The template remains available in Config, ready to apply again whenever you want it.

ChoosePick another wallpaper in Wallpaper Engine or set one in Awl.
ReloadUse the reload control in Quick to read the current Wallpaper Engine selection.
ReapplyClick the template again later to restore its wallpaper and colors.
